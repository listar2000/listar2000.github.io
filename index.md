I am a third-year Ph.D. student at UChicago [Data Science Institute](https://datascience.uchicago.edu/) (DSI). At DSI, I have the pleasure of working with [Prof. Haifeng Xu](https://www.haifeng-xu.com/) and [Prof. Nikolaos Ignatiadis](https://nignatiadis.github.io/). Previously, I finished my M.S. in Statistics under the supervision of [Prof. Daniel Sanz-Alonso](https://sites.google.com/a/uchicago.edu/sanz-alonso/). I am also a part of the [AER Lab](https://musslick.github.io/AER_website/About.html) under the mentorship of [Prof. Sebastian Musslick](https://smusslick.com/). Here is my [Google Scholar](https://scholar.google.com/citations?user=XBTlUzUAAAAJ&hl=en&oi=sra).

I work on **reinforcement learning for LLM agents** and on **statistical methods for noisy data**. In practice, that has meant a few concrete things:

- **Training agents.** I am a core contributor to [rLLM](https://github.com/rllm-org/rllm), an open-source framework for agentic RL, and I have worked on making reasoning models stop overthinking ([ShorterBetter](https://arxiv.org/abs/2504.21370)).
- **Evaluating models.** I help build [Prophet Arena](https://www.prophetarena.co/), which tests LLMs on forecasting real-world events, and I have studied what benchmarks actually measure and how alignment narrows what models generate.
- **Empirical Bayes.** I develop estimators that combine a small amount of reliable data with a large amount of cheap ML predictions, with guarantees on how well they do ([PAS](https://arxiv.org/abs/2502.14166)).

From March to September 2026, I was a Research Intern at [Snowflake AI Research](https://www.snowflake.com/en/product/ai/ai-research/), working on post-training agentic systems. I am currently a Member of Technical Staff (MTS) Intern at **Mobius Intelligence**, building synthetic environments for agentic RL.

_More about me_: even earlier, I graduated from UC Berkeley [EECS](https://eecs.berkeley.edu/) & [Statistics](https://statistics.berkeley.edu/). I enjoyed working as a software engineer in an "earlier life".
